Phase 2: Document Remediation

Audits are your first service. Document remediation is your second — and for many contractors the steadier, more repeatable bread-and-butter work. It’s in constant demand, and especially valued in government, where every public PDF is supposed to be accessible by law.

What document remediation is

Remediation means taking a document that already exists — a PDF, Word file, PowerPoint, or Excel sheet — and making it fully usable by someone with a screen reader. Most documents are built for the eye only: a screen reader often finds no headings, no reading order, images with no description, tables it can’t parse. Your job is to add the structure underneath so the document reads correctly out loud.

What the work involves

Tags and reading order — so assistive tech reads in the right sequence.

Real headings and lists — so a screen-reader user can skim and navigate.

Alt text for images and charts.

Table headers — so rows and columns make sense aloud.

Document language, title, and bookmarks.

The standards are WCAG and PDF/UA, the accessibility standard specific to PDFs. Know what “good” looks like and how to get a document there.

The tools

Microsoft Office’s built-in Accessibility Checker (Word, PowerPoint, Excel) — free, and where most remediation starts.

Adobe Acrobat Pro — the standard tool for tagging and fixing PDFs (a paid subscription; a business expense once you’re earning).

PAC (PDF Accessibility Checker) — a free tool that tests a finished PDF against PDF/UA.

The credential: IAAP ADS — a build-toward goal

The matching certification is the Accessible Document Specialist (ADS) from the IAAP — a proctored exam (75 questions, 70% to pass). But ADS asks for roughly one to two years of hands-on experience first. So learn the skill now, offer remediation as a service, accumulate projects — and earn ADS once you’ve got the hours. You can bill for the work well before you hold the certificate. Confirm fees on the IAAP ADS page → https://www.accessibilityassociation.org/ads-exam (around $430 members / $530 otherwise as of June 2026).

For this module, your proof isn’t the certificate — it’s a demonstration. Take a short, messy sample document, remediate it, and upload the before and after.

Quick recap

Remediation makes existing documents usable with assistive technology, measured against WCAG and PDF/UA.

Tools are mostly free or standard: Office’s checker, Adobe Acrobat Pro, the free PAC checker.

IAAP ADS needs ~1–2 years’ experience — build the skill and bill for it now, earn ADS later.
