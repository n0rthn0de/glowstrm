The Pathway · Part A · Module 5

PART A · THE RELATIONSHIP · ~30 MINUTES

What GlowStrm Offers You

By now you know the opportunity and how to build a business around it. This module makes the other half concrete: what GlowStrm actually is, and exactly how you’d work with us. Knowing the destination is what keeps motivation steady through the credentialing work ahead.

What GlowStrm is

GlowStrm is a consulting collective — a hub where Black women professionals collaborate to take on contracts none of us could land alone. It’s not an employer, and you’re not an employee. You own your business; GlowStrm helps fill it with real client work and stands behind you with a recognized name, shared standards, and a community doing the same work.

Think of it as the difference between knocking on every door by yourself and being part of a group that answers the door together. You still do your own work and own your own business — but you’re not alone in finding clients or figuring things out.

How you’re brought on

When you’re credentialed and ready, becoming a GlowStrm contractor runs on a small set of documents — already built and reviewed by our legal coverage (LegalShield), so you’re not signing anything improvised:

An offer letter that lays out the working relationship in plain terms.

A mutual NDA that protects both you and GlowStrm — and the clients you’ll serve.

A retainer agreement that sets how you’re engaged and paid.

You sign these as your own business, contracting with GlowStrm — not as an employee being hired. That distinction is the whole point: you’re building something you own.

How work reaches you

As client projects come in — a website audit, a batch of documents to remediate — GlowStrm routes them to credentialed contractors. You deliver under the GlowStrm umbrella, invoice through your own LLC, and build a track record. Early, simpler projects come first; as your credentials and experience grow, so does the work and the rate. You’ll see exactly how that first engagement works in the final module.

The partnership, plainly

You bring the credential and the work; GlowStrm brings the name, the clients, the standards, and the community. You keep ownership of your business and your earnings. That’s the deal — and it’s why the credentialing work in the next part is worth the hours: there’s a real, concrete destination on the other side.

Quick recap

GlowStrm is a consulting collective — you own your business; it helps fill it with work.

You’re brought on via an offer letter, mutual NDA, and retainer agreement (LegalShield-reviewed) — as your own business.

Credentialed contractors get real client work routed to them, delivered under GlowStrm and invoiced through their own LLC.

What’s next

That’s the end of Part A. Part B makes the work itself real — and then the Checkpoint opens the credentialing path.
