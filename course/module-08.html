The Pathway · Part C · Module 8

PART C · FOUNDATION · ~20 HOURS (PLAN 2–4 WEEKS)

Foundation: Learn the Rules

This is where the real coursework begins. Now you learn the rules everyone in this field is measured against — the standard called WCAG — properly, from the people who wrote it.

What you’re doing in this module

You’re completing one free foundations course: the W3C’s “Introduction to Web Accessibility,” the most respected starting point in the field. Plan about 20 hours of focused work over two to four weeks.

Take it here: W3C “Introduction to Web Accessibility” (W3Cx on edX) → https://www.edx.org/learn/web-accessibility/the-world-wide-web-consortium-w3c-introduction-to-web-accessibility · details at https://www.w3.org/WAI/courses/foundations-course/

The course is free to audit. For about $99 you can add a verified certificate — and we recommend it: it’s your first real, uploadable credential, and the proof you’ll submit here. Check the current price on edX before you pay.

What you’ll actually learn

The four POUR principles in depth — Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, Robust.

What WCAG “success criteria” are, and how to read one.

The conformance levels — A, AA, AAA — and why “WCAG 2.1 AA” is the line.

How disabilities, barriers, and accessibility features connect.

You don’t need to memorize the standard — you need to open it, find the relevant criterion, and understand what it asks. That’s the foundation of every audit you’ll do.

A word on pace

Twenty hours sounds like a lot until you divide it. Five hours a week finishes it in about a month; ten a week, in two. Put it on your calendar like an appointment — a steady hour most days beats one heroic weekend. Optional and free: Section508.gov (https://www.section508.gov) grounds the standard in the U.S. legal context you’ll work in.

Quick recap

Complete the free W3C course — about 20 hours.

Learn WCAG properly: POUR, success criteria, A/AA/AAA.

Upload your certificate (the ~$99 verified one is worth it) to complete the module.
