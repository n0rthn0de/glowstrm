Institutional services.

Most institutions do not have a content problem. They have a coherence problem that shows up as a content problem. The strategic plan says one thing, the website says another, and the person answering the phone says a third. Every version was written by someone competent. Together they do not add up.

GlowStrm does the message-clarity work that has to happen before content, campaigns, or public engagement. These are the services scoped for departments, programs, and agencies, with pricing structured so you can see where an engagement lands before you start a requisition.

How the work is structured

Benchmark. Build. Re-measure.

These three services are one cycle, and each is purchasable on its own. You establish a benchmark before the work, you do the work, and you measure against the benchmark afterward. Most communications work skips the first and the third, which is why so much of it can never say whether it worked.

How the range works

Where a service shows a range, the rate is identical at both ends. What changes is how many hours the work takes, and that depends almost entirely on the condition of what you hand over at intake. An institution that arrives organized lands at the bottom of the range. One that hands over scattered materials across a dozen owners with no inventory lands nearer the top, because reconstructing that record is real hours. You are told which end you are tracking toward before the work begins, not after.
