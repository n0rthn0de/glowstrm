How universities can engage

Most departments can bring GlowStrm in directly — workshops and consulting are scoped as fixed-fee engagements sized to fit departmental delegated-purchase limits, no formal solicitation required. Virginia public institutions can purchase through eVA, contracts awarded by one Virginia institution can be shared through cooperative purchasing, and GlowStrm also teams under prime contractors' existing vehicles.
